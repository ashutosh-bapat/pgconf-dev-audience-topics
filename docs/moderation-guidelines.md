# Moderation and organizer guidelines

These guidelines support consistent, respectful moderation for community-requested topic discussions.

## Duplicate handling

- Ask participants to search before posting.
- When duplicates appear, close or redirect newer threads to one canonical discussion.
- Leave a short note with a link so contributors know where to continue.

## Respectful discussion expectations

- Keep discussion constructive, respectful, and relevant to the topic.
- Encourage specific feedback (use cases, audience needs, learning goals).
- Remove or de-escalate personal attacks, harassment, or off-topic behavior according to repository/community standards.

## Handling suggested speakers

- Treat speaker suggestions as ideas, not commitments.
- Do not imply a nominated speaker has agreed to present.
- Encourage suggestions with supporting context (prior talks, writing, project work).
- Avoid pressure tactics or repeated tagging of individuals.

## Interpreting votes and support

- Upvotes and comments are important demand signals.
- Votes are one input among several; final decisions also consider program balance, conference goals, and speaker availability.
- Communicate clearly that high support does not guarantee acceptance.
